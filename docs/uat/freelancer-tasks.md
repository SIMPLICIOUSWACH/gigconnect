# Freelancer task script

Read each task to the participant as written. Do not show them where to click. Ask them to think aloud. Record each
task in results.md: completed alone, with help, or not completed; the time; and anything they tried that did not work.

## F1. Create your account

"Sign up as a freelancer with your own email, and verify it."

- Done when: the account exists, the email is verified and the participant is signed in.

## F2. Set up your profile

"Add the skills you really have, between two and four of them, and a one-line bio."

- Done when: the skills and bio show on the profile page.
- Note: the recommendations depend on these skills. If the participant has no skill the platform lists, note which
  skill they looked for.

## F3. Find a gig you would like

"Find a gig you would consider applying for, using search or the filters."

- Done when: the participant opens a gig that fits what they said they wanted.

## F4. Understand a recommendation

"Look at the gigs recommended for you on the Browse Gigs page. Pick one and tell me why you think it was recommended
to you."

- Done when: the participant explains it from the "Matches your skills" line (the skills they share with the gig), or
  says it is based on their skills.
- Note what they think the recommendation is based on, in their words, even if it is wrong. That is a finding.

## F5. Apply to a gig

"Apply to a gig you would really consider, with a short cover letter."

- Done when: the application shows under My Applications as pending.

## F6. Check your application

"Find out where your application stands."

- Done when: the participant finds the application and reads its status.

## F7. Rate your recommendations

Follow relevance-rating.md. This is part of the session, not a task to time.
