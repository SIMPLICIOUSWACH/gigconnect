# Client task script

Read each task to the participant as written. Do not show them where to click. Ask them to think aloud. Record each
task in results.md: completed alone, with help, or not completed; the time; and anything they tried that did not work.

## C1. Create your account

"Sign up as a client with your own email, and verify it."

- Done when: the account exists, the email is verified and the participant is signed in.

## C2. Post a gig

"Post a gig you could imagine needing done: a title, a description, a budget, a deadline and the skills it needs."

- Done when: the gig shows on the participant's dashboard and on Browse Gigs.
- Observer: once it is posted, sign in as the prepared freelancer account in another browser and apply to it, so there
  is an applicant for C3. Use a freelancer whose skills match the gig.

## C3. Review your applicants

"Find the people who applied to your gig and look at one application."

- Done when: the participant opens the applicants list and reads an application.

## C4. Read the skill match

"For that applicant, tell me how well their skills match your gig, and what the page says that is based on."

- Done when: the participant reads the skill match (strong, good or some skill overlap, or that there is no score) and
  explains it from the shared skills.
- Note how they read the band. Do they take it as a score of the person, or of the skill overlap only?

## C5. Decide

"Move the application on: shortlist the applicant, or reject them."

- Done when: the status changes and the participant can see that it did.
